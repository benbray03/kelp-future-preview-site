How to Use the Map

  • Choose a species using the buttons above the map.
  • Select a variable to display: either the modeled kelp density, or one of the underlying environmental predictors.
  • Use the year slider (or press play) to step through projected years.
  • The side panel plots update to summarize the currently displayed layer.
  • The orange dashed line marks the 50% density latitude: half of the species' predicted kelp density lies south of it and half north. The grey dotted line shows where it was in the first projection year.
